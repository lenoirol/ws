# WS by Phu Huy Nguyen

Make classroom-ready **Word Search**, **Word Scramble** and **Crossword** worksheets in your browser, alone or combined.

Type or paste a word list, pick a style, and get a clean printable sheet with a matching answer key in seconds. Puzzles are made on your device and the words you type are never uploaded. An account is optional and only needed to save projects.

## What you can do

- **Word Search**: automatic or custom grid sizes from 5×5 up to 100×100, rows and columns set independently. Choose which of the eight directions words may use, from "easy" (across and down) to "hard" (all eight), with crossing words and filler-letter options.
- **Word Scramble**: every word is scrambled on its own and never comes out unchanged. Keep or change the first letter, and reveal starter letters in the blanks.
- **Crossword**: built from your word list with numbered cells and Across/Down clue lists. Optional letter counts and word bank; with no clues it prints a word bank so the puzzle can still be solved. Long lists move the clues to a second page instead of shrinking the grid.
- **Combine**: pair any two puzzles (Word Search + Scramble, Word Search + Crossword, or Crossword + Scramble) from one word list. Each puzzle gets its own page, all pages go into one PDF, PNG set or print job, with the puzzle sheets first and the answer keys after.
- **Clues**: add a clue after any word (`word | clue`, or paste two columns from a spreadsheet) and show words, clues, or both.
- **Answer keys**: circled words with a dot on the first letter for Word Search, filled-in blanks for Word Scramble.
- **Many copies at once**: generate up to 50 different puzzles in one go, each with its own seed, so every student gets a different sheet.
- **Repeatable results**: every puzzle has a seed, so the same words and seed always give the same sheet.
- **Your page, your way**: title, name line, footer, A4, Letter or A3 in portrait or landscape.

## Export and share

- Save as **PNG** (150, 300 or 600 DPI), or as a crisp vector **PDF**.
- **Print** straight from the app with exact page sizing.
- On phones and tablets, send the result through the system share sheet.
## Accounts and privacy

Everything above works without signing in. Creating an account (email and password, with password reset) is only needed to save projects.

Each visit, signed in or not, records one usage row: when it started, the browser's user agent, and counts of how many times you exported PNG or PDF, printed, shared or regenerated. The words, titles and anything else you type are never sent. Only administrators can read these rows.

Signed-in users can save the current worksheet, reopen it from **My Projects**, and move several projects to the trash at once. The trash keeps them until you restore them or delete them for good.

**Forgot password** does not send an email. The request goes to the administrators, and an approval sets the default password (`123456`), signs the person out everywhere and asks them to choose a new password at the next sign-in.

Administrators get an **Admin** entry in the account menu (with a badge when requests are waiting):

- **Users**: search every account, see the last visit and device, open a person's page to edit their name, email and role, browse their saved projects (never their trash) and open any of them as a copy.
- **Requests**: approve or dismiss password requests.
- **Activity**: every visit, guests included, with the exact date and time, the device (for example Safari on iPhone) and what was exported.

Passwords are never visible to anyone. The database enforces all of this, not just the screen.
